How the gate decides what's a match
1 · Appearance A frozen OSNet embedding scores visual similarity between two tracklets across cameras — appearanceSimilarity, 0–1.
2 · Transit plausibility A log-likelihood over observed travel times between that camera pair scores whether the gap between sightings is physically consistent — transitPlausibility, 0–1.
3 · Gate A logistic gate combines both signals into one accept/reject call — catching cases a fixed appearance-only threshold gets wrong in either direction. See the amber notes on hop cards below.
